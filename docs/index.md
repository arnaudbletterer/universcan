## What Makes It Special

- 🎯 **Only 1 Big Button to Scan:** No complex menus. Put your paper in, click **Scan Now**, and watch your pages appear on screen.
- 🖨️ **Works with Almost Any Scanner:** Samsung, HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, Xerox, Lexmark, Kyocera, Ricoh, and more.
- 🚫 **No Drivers or Accounts Needed:** No 500 MB printer driver CDs, no passwords to create, no ads, and no tracking.
- 🔄 **Crystal-Clear 2-Sided Scanning:** If your scanner only scans one side at a time, UniverScan guides you with a simple picture to turn the pages, and automatically puts them in the right order (1, 2, 3...).
- 🔒 **100% Private on Your Computer:** Your scans stay safely on your computer in your own Documents folder. Nothing is ever sent to the cloud.
- 💻 **Runs on Old and New Computers Alike:** Works on modern Windows 11 and 10, older Windows 7 (32-bit and 64-bit), Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, and Linux.

## 3 Easy Steps to Scan

```
[ 1. Put Paper In ]  ──▶  [ 2. Click "Scan Now" ]  ──▶  [ 3. Click "Save as PDF" ]
```

1. **Put your paper in:** Place your sheets in the top tray, or put a page on the glass under the lid.
2. **Click "Scan Now":** Your scanner starts immediately and pages appear live on your screen.
3. **Click "Save as PDF":** Your document is saved neatly into your `Documents/Scans` folder.
