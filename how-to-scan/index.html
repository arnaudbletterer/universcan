How to Scan with UniverScan 📑¶

Scanning with UniverScan is as simple as making a photocopy. Here is everything you need to know.

1. Single Page (Under the Lid)¶

Use the scanner glass for single sheets, receipts, books, thick documents, or ID cards:

  1. Lift the scanner lid.
  2. Place your page face-down on the glass. Align it with the arrow corner (usually top-left).
  3. Close the lid gently.
  4. In UniverScan, ensure Under Lid (Glass) is selected.
  5. Click the big blue Scan Now button.
  6. In a few seconds, your page appears on screen!

2. Multi-Page Documents (Top Tray Feeder)¶

If your scanner has a paper tray on top (an Automatic Document Feeder):

  1. Stack your sheets neatly.
  2. Slide them face-up into the top tray until you hear a soft chime or feel the paper stop.
  3. Notice that UniverScan automatically selects Top Tray (Feeder)!
  4. Click Scan Now.
  5. Your scanner feeds each sheet through one by one, and every page pops up on your screen live as it scans.

Helpful Tip: If you leave the top tray empty, UniverScan automatically uses the glass under the lid so you never get an error!

3. Organizing Your Scanned Pages¶

Once your pages are on screen:

  • Rotate a Page: If a page was scanned upside down, click the ↺ or ↷ icon on that page card.
  • Rotate All Pages: Click Rotate Left or Rotate Right on the top toolbar to turn all pages together.
  • Reorder Pages: Click and drag any page card left or right to put them in the exact order you want.
  • Inspect Full Screen: Click anywhere on a page image to zoom in and check small text or numbers.
  • Delete a Page: If you scanned a blank page by accident, click the little 🗑 (Trash) button on that card.

4. Saving Your PDF¶

When you are satisfied with your pages:

  1. UniverScan automatically suggests a clear name with today's date and time (for example, Scan_2026-09-27_1015).
  2. If you want to give it a custom name (like Tax_Receipt_2026), just type it in the Document Name box.
  3. Click the big Save as PDF button.
  4. Your PDF is saved instantly to your computer's Documents/Scans folder, and will open right away for you to view or attach to an email!
  5. To see all your saved scans anytime, just click Open Scans Folder.
